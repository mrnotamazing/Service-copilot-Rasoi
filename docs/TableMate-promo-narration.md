# TableMate promo film: narration script

A story, not a caption track: it follows one busy Friday and says what the pictures can't. Warm storyteller voice (ElevenLabs "George"), soft music bed that ducks under the voice, one small sound effect per scene. The source of truth is `scripts/film/narration.json` (a test keeps it in step with `src/pages/intro/scenes.tsx`).

| Scene | Narration | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Open | Every great dinner is a small miracle. Someone makes it happen. | restaurant murmur, cutlery chime |
| 2 · Pressure | Friday, seven-forty. Twelve tables, one tray, and a hundred tiny things that mustn't be forgotten. | kitchen clatter |
| 3 · Slip | Then, the water never arrives. Nobody's fault. Just a very human, very busy night. | glass clink, soft "oops" |
| 4 · Next three | Meet TableMate. A quiet friend on your phone, whispering the next three moves. Suggestions, never orders. | three chimes |
| 5 · Safety | Allergy on table four? It catches that before the plate leaves the pass. Some mistakes can't be taken back. | alert ping, success chime |
| 6 · Fair delays | And when things run late, it tells the truth: floor, or kitchen. No blame games. Just clarity. | soft whoosh |
| 7 · Manager | Managers get the whole room at a glance. Flow, not scoreboards. Support, never surveillance. | gentle ding |
| 8 · Practice | Dreading a tough guest? Rehearse it first, privately, with a coach who actually talks back. | two chat pops |
| 9 · Everyone | In your language. On your phone. For everyone on the team. | warm chime |
| 10 · Close | TableMate. Be there for every guest. | bell sparkle |
